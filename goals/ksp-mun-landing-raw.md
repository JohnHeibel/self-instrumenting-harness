# Goal: land a kerbal on the Mun and bring them home

The program is **Kerbal Space Program**, in a sandbox game: every part is unlocked, and money and science don't matter. You start at the Space Center with four kerbals and no craft. The game's example craft are not included, so you design and build your own.

**Objective:** land a crewed vessel on the **Mun**, then bring that kerbal back alive to the surface of **Kerbin** (landed or splashed down).

**Done when** a kerbal who landed on the Mun is back on Kerbin's surface.

**Progress** is measured by these steps, in order: first launch (above 1 km), space (above 70 km), orbit of Kerbin (periapsis above 70 km), Mun encounter, orbit of the Mun, landing on the Mun (any craft), a kerbal on the Mun, and that kerbal back on Kerbin. A grader reads them from the game's own state. **Tokens** are everything you spend.

**Play as a player does.** You fly with the keyboard and mouse: throttle, pitch, yaw, roll, SAS, staging, the map view, maneuver nodes, time warp, reverting a flight and quicksave/quickload are all the game's own features, and allowed.

Not allowed: the cheats in the debug menu (Alt+F12: infinite fuel, no crash damage, set orbit and the rest), setting orbits, positions or velocities directly, adding fuel or other resources, changing parts' stats or the game's physics or config files, spawning vessels in flight, and editing save files. A grader flags the cheats, orbital energy that rises more than the vessel's engines allow, and vessels that appear in flight from nothing. Any of these voids the run.
